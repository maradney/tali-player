# Content & Takedown Policy

This project is a **generic IPTV client**. It plays streams from subscriptions
and playlists that users supply themselves. The application and this
repository:

- do **not** provide, host, sell, bundle, or index any TV channels, movies,
  or series;
- ship with **no content sources** — no default servers, playlists, provider
  directories, or discovery features of any kind;
- collect **no telemetry** — nothing about what users watch, search,
  download, or connect to ever leaves their device.

Because the app contains no content and no pointers to content, there is
nothing content-related to take down from this repository. Nevertheless:

## If you are a rightsholder

If you believe this project facilitates infringement of your rights in some
specific way, open a GitHub issue or contact the maintainer directly. The
policy of this project is to **respond promptly and cooperatively** to any
good-faith complaint from a rightsholder or their agent — including removing
or changing any feature, text, or asset that is reasonably identified as
infringing — rather than to contest it.

## What will not be added

The project permanently excludes:

- any bundled, demo, or trial IPTV provider or playlist;
- provider/channel marketplaces, directories, or "find IPTV" features;
- discovery or brute-forcing of servers or credentials;
- credential sharing or generation;
- any telemetry about watched content.
